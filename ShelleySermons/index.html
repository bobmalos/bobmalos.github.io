Calling All My Artistic Friends

Thank you for your willingness to share your talent and skills! You are invited to create a work of art (or use one you have already made) that relates to one or two of the sermons listed below. A picture of your artwork will be printed in the book I'm putting together of my best sermons. Sadly, there is no financial remuneration for your artistic work, however, you will be a published artist when the book is done and you will have my deepest gratitude. Your work of art remains your property and you can do with it whatever you like. I'm just glad to have a copy of your work for the book.

All of the sermons are listed below as well as a brief description of each one. Click on a link to open a sermon. Look over the sermons that interest you, select one or two, and then claim that sermon in the spreadsheet posted below. Once someone has placed their name next to a sermon on the spreadsheet, it is no longer available. Use your chosen sermons as idea starters and have a blast creating an artwork that goes with the sermon.

All the artwork needs to be completed by the end of 2026. You can create new work or use an existing piece. Once it is done I will need a high resolution photo ; a cellphone photo will work just fine. Your artwork can be in any medium as long as you can take a photo of it.

NOTE - this is a LIVE spreadsheet so please be careful to just add your name next to the sermon(s) you want.

Abundance What does it mean to have an abundant life? How do we increase our abundance? Together we will examine the steps we can take to make our lives fuller and richer.
Bible Basic Bootcamp Some people spend a lifetime trying to escape the Bible. Others could care less about it. Many are experiencing extreme religion’s attempt to force their understanding of “The Good Book” on to all areas of society. Today, we will look at the history of the Bible, how it came to be and how it is used. We will uncover solid and surprising answers to those who declare the Bible is Inspired, Inerrant, and Infallible.
Describing The Indescribable What do we mean when we talk about God? Often the idea of “God” is presented as though there is a common understanding and we all agree what it means. Yet there are many different ways to describe the Divine. If you are an agnostic, join us to learn more about what is uncertain. If you are an atheist, join us to learn more about what you don’t believe. If you are a theist, join us to discover how complicated it is to describe the indescribable!
Emotion and Religion "Emotions are a particular way of knowing. They carry insight and wisdom. Emotions get in the way. They distort our thinking and make a mess. They are wonderful and feel terrible. Emotions, religion and spirituality intersect - enriching or diminishing each other. Join us as we explore what science and spirituality have to say about emotions."
God In The Feed Trough Christmas sermon. God shows up when things are at their worst.
Going On Using the story of Elijah under the broom tree as an example, we look at instances in life when people are given exactly what they need by way of unlikely angels.
Holy Shadow Examines some of the ways we are blessed by the elderly. Invitation to contemplate who we want to be when we are old.
How Many UUs Does It Take to Screw In a Lightbulb What does it mean to be a Unitarian Universalist? Structured around UU jokes.
