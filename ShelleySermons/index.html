Irregular Insights: Sermons With A Different Perspective

Calling All My Artistic Friends

Thank you for your willingness to share your talent and skills! You are invited to create a work of art (or use one you have already made) that relates to one or two of the sermons listed below. A picture of your artwork will be printed in the book I'm putting together of my best sermons. Sadly, there is no financial remuneration for your artistic work, however, you will be a published artist when the book is done and you will have my deepest gratitude. Your work of art remains your property and you can do with it whatever you like. I'm just glad to have a copy of your work for the book.

All of the sermons are listed below as well as a brief description of each one. Click on a link to open a sermon. Look over the sermons that interest you, select one or two, and then claim that sermon in the spreadsheet posted below. Once someone has placed their name next to a sermon on the spreadsheet, it is no longer available. Use your chosen sermons as idea starters and have a blast creating an artwork that goes with the sermon.

All the artwork needs to be completed by the end of 2026. You can create new work or use an existing piece. Once it is done I will need a high resolution photo ; a cellphone photo will work just fine. Your artwork can be in any medium as long as you can take a photo of it.

NOTE - this is a LIVE spreadsheet so please be careful to just add your name next to the sermon(s) you want.

Abundance What does it mean to have an abundant life? How do we increase our abundance? Together we will examine the steps we can take to make our lives fuller and richer.
Bible Basic Bootcamp Some people spend a lifetime trying to escape the Bible. Others could care less about it. Many are experiencing extreme religion’s attempt to force their understanding of “The Good Book” on to all areas of society. Today, we will look at the history of the Bible, how it came to be and how it is used. We will uncover solid and surprising answers to those who declare the Bible is Inspired, Inerrant, and Infallible.
Describing The Indescribable What do we mean when we talk about God? Often the idea of “God” is presented as though there is a common understanding and we all agree what it means. Yet there are many different ways to describe the Divine. If you are an agnostic, join us to learn more about what is uncertain. If you are an atheist, join us to learn more about what you don’t believe. If you are a theist, join us to discover how complicated it is to describe the indescribable!
Emotion and Religion "Emotions are a particular way of knowing. They carry insight and wisdom. Emotions get in the way. They distort our thinking and make a mess. They are wonderful and feel terrible. Emotions, religion and spirituality intersect - enriching or diminishing each other. Join us as we explore what science and spirituality have to say about emotions."
God In The Feed Trough Christmas sermon. God shows up when things are at their worst.
Going On Using the story of Elijah under the broom tree as an example, we look at instances in life when people are given exactly what they need by way of unlikely angels.
Holy Shadow Examines some of the ways we are blessed by the elderly. Invitation to contemplate who we want to be when we are old.
How Many UUs Does It Take to Screw In a Lightbulb What does it mean to be a Unitarian Universalist? Structured around UU jokes.
How Much is That Nice Church In the Window A brief history of the relationship between money and religion. The importance of giving financially to the church. “Give until it feels great!”
Illness Illness changes us and how we interact with our world. In exploring the experience of being out for surgery, the preacher discovers she understands her elder nursing home residents in deeper ways.
In Praise Of Regret Some boldly state that they have no regrets. That’s too bad! Instead of denying regrets or storing them in locked compartments of our psyche, let us bring them to the table. Our regrets have benefits. If examined, they have gifts. Join us as we welcome our regrets to the table. Perhaps after getting to know them, we won’t find them so regrettable!
In The Twinkling Of An Eye We are warned to be careful. We can destroy relationships and do great harm in any careless instance. We must be ever vigilant. Let us toy with the possibility that goodness and light can also become present in our lives during any miraculous instant.
Listen Carefully Almost every person will tell you that they are good drivers, have a great sense of humor, and that they’re excellent listeners. Many of us have been taught the basic skills of how to be a good communicator. It can be a lot of work. Join us this Sunday as we play with a new way to listen that is easier and more effective than what you were probably taught. It is a simple and enjoyable way to connect deeply with others. Listen up!
Love Conquers All Is love the most powerful force in the universe? This sermon explores the idea of love overcoming us rather than overcoming all hardships. The changing power of love is also linked to Process Theology.
Mulligan In this serious business of life, we have the surprising opportunity of “do-overs.” As adults, we often find ourselves in familiar situations. Each time we do, we have another chance to do it differently. In golf, a “do-over” is a mulligan. In theology, we call it grace.
Pain 1 The Problem of Pain "Life is Suffering" according to the Buddha. Pain is everywhere. It impacts every aspect of our human lives - physical, spiritual, emotional, financial, relational - it can even change our personality and identity. Unbelievable amounts of money, time, and energy are spent every year in an effort to escape pain, with varying degrees of success. Our culture, especially marketing, suggests that people are wrong to experience suffering, and they just happened to have the thing that will make that pain go away! Yet the truth is, we are all hurting. Join us this Sunday as we examine pain and how it affects each of us. It should be a relatively pain-free service!
Pain 2 The Purpose of Pain "When we hurt, we often ask "Why? Why did this happen to me?" We don't usually want an answer; there is no answer that would satisfy. Later, when we've had some time and experienced some healing, we discover we have gained something from our troubles. Pain does far more than tell us to take our hand off a hot stove. It is important for our individual and collective survival, our spiritual growth and even our education. Together we will explore the purpose of pain, gain appreciation for this backward gift, and understand that if there was no pain, we would be in a world of hurt!
Pain 3 Pain and Religion Religion and pain have a complicated relationship. They attract each other, repel each other, create each other and deny each other. There are many different ways religions understand discomfort, disease, and distress. Join us for this third and final sermon on pain. Together we will examine how the convoluted layers of our beliefs and our struggles become intertwined and what we can do about it.
Quitting We live in a world of winners - people who make it to the top of the mountain, the top of their game, the top of the best-seller list. People who demonstrate that if you just work hard enough, keep believing, and don't give up, eventually, inevitably, you will win. This sermon is for the people who didn't win. Their big dream did not come true. This is for the quitters.
Scars Unsightly scars? There are creams to help fade scars, applications to lessen their formation, makeup to hide them and clothes to cover them. Many people feel shame about their scars. Shall we look at them?
Seeing The Positive In Negative People Difficult people vex us. Yet spiritually, they are worth their weight in gold. Together we explore what goodness can come from such frustrating situations.
The Art of the Apology Eating Humble Pie With Grace There are few things more healing than a thoughtful apology, and few things more disappointing than a poor one. Learning to apologize well is a deeply important skill. Join us as we explore what it means to apologize and how to do it with grace.
The Spirituality of Mediocrity Who wants to be mediocre? Or perhaps worse, who wants to be seen as mediocre? Ordinary, run-of-the-mill, nothing to write home about. Or even lower than that - second-rate, low-grade, a hack. Some people, ironically, have been told to “work on their perfectionism” - meaning “get worse at being perfect.” Letting go of the need for perfectionism is an important aspect of spiritual maturity. Yet, striving for mediocrity is a difficult goal to wrap our heads around. Is there something to being mediocre besides freedom from the tyranny of perfectionism? Together, in perhaps a mediocre sermon, let us explore the spiritual benefits of not being amazing!
Vessels And Cables Understanding the church as a community of mutual support, allowing room for individuals to “break” as they need to. A cable is stronger than a chain.
What Don’t You Believe In There are over 10,000 religions today. In addition to the major religions we all know, there are belief systems associated with ancestors, animals, energy, and stars. People believe in many things. Equally, there are so many things not to believe in, and there are many ways not to believe. Just as there are different denominations of faith, there are different types of non-faith. Today we are going to examine different ways to be an atheist. It is not as simple as saying “I don’t believe in God.” Join us today as we examine the different ways people don’t believe.
